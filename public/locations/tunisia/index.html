Wedding photographer in Tunisia

Yesser Barka is a wedding photographer for couples getting married in Tunisia. He lives in Sousse, on the Sahel coast, and he travels for the wedding: Greater Tunis, the Cap Bon coast, and the Sahel are the places already on this site. Couples searching for a photographe mariage en Tunisie are booking one person for the full day, not a posed hour and not a roster of strangers.

Where does Yesser Barka photograph weddings in Tunisia?

From Sousse. There is no second office in Tunis or anywhere else. A date in another city means he drives, and an early call time away from the Sahel includes the night before. The places below are the ones named on the homepage calendar.

Tunis and the north coast

A Tunis wedding usually means moving. Preparations can start in a family house in Ariana, El Menzah, or La Marsa, then the party continues in Gammarth or among the white walls of Sidi Bou Said. The drive from Sousse is about 140 kilometres on the A1, close to an hour and forty minutes without traffic. The published Tunis story is Chayma and Sami. The day itself is written up on wedding photographer in Tunis.

Sidi Bou Said

Sidi Bou Said sits on the cliff above the Gulf of Tunis, about twenty kilometres northeast of the city. Noon on the white walls blows the highlights out, so portraits belong in a doorway or after four. It is part of a Greater Tunis day, not a separate studio.

Hammamet

Hammamet is on the Cap Bon coast, between Tunis and Sousse, so it does not need the capital’s evening traffic or a Sahel sunrise plan. Resort hotels hold the whole day in one property more often than a Tunis wedding does. The published Hammamet photographs are Rami and Cyrine.

Nabeul

Nabeul is the next town along Cap Bon from Hammamet, a short hop rather than a highway day. The published Nabeul photographs are Laura and Yosri. If the morning is in Nabeul and dinner is in Hammamet, that gap has to be in the note when you book, because the soft light does not wait for the cars.

Sousse and the Sahel

A Sousse wedding stays on the Sahel. The medina, the Boujaafar corniche, and Port El Kantaoui are minutes apart, and the day does not depend on crossing the capital. He already lives there, so there is no dawn drive. That page is wedding photographer in Sousse, and the published story is Erij and Bechir.

What about a wedding somewhere else in Tunisia?

If the date is free, he travels. Tell him the town, the first address, and the time guests are told to arrive on the inquiry form. He does not keep a photographer in every governorate. One person leaves Sousse, photographs the day, and comes back.
